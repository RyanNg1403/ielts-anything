# IELTS Listening: construction rules

Checked 11 October 2026. Shared by Academic and General Training. Confidence meanings: [sources.md](sources.md). Printed page locators refer to the 33-page official booklet [P-L]. Extracts in that booklet are independent samples, not one concatenatable 40-question test.

## 1. Official format

| Property / construction rule | Confidence | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Four parts, ten scored questions each, 40 overall; approximately 30 minutes of test listening; recordings played once. | confirmed-official | [F-L] |
| Part 1: a two-person conversation in an everyday/social setting. | confirmed-official | [F-L] |
| Part 2: one main speaker in an everyday/social setting, such as local facilities or a public talk. | confirmed-official | [F-L] |
| Part 3: education/training discussion between two main speakers, potentially involving a tutor; not a requirement for exactly three voices. Partner practice guidance describes up to four speakers. | confirmed-official current format; partner guidance indexed only for maximum | [F-L], [L-SPEAKERS] |
| Part 4: one speaker on an academic subject; a lecture/talk rather than a social dialogue. | confirmed-official | [F-L] |
| Answers follow information order in the recording. Multi-answer sets may be keyed either order within the set. | confirmed-official chronology; confirmed-by-papers unordered exception | [F-L], [P-L] pp.7, 13 |
| Accent variety can include British, Australian, New Zealand and North American English. Every test need not contain one of each. | confirmed-official variety; unverified exact quota | [F-L], [DEV] |
| Historical paper simulation: ten extra minutes to transfer answers. Computer simulation: two final minutes to check typed answers. These are different delivery profiles. | confirmed-official | [GUIDE], [COMPUTER] |
| “Parts” terminology and no worked Part 1 opening example should be used for contemporary standard practice. | confirmed-official partner guidance | [CHANGE-2020], effective January 2020 |
| No publicly fixed script word count, speech rate, per-part duration or accent allocation was found. | unverified for fixed values | [F-L], [DEV] do not publish these quotas |

Recommended progression: concrete transactions → public information/spatial explanation → educational negotiation/evaluation → sustained academic explanation. This preserves the specified contexts without making all of Part 1 artificially slow or all of Part 4 lexically obscure. **Confidence: confirmed-official for contexts; unverified design recommendation for exact difficulty progression**, [F-L], [DEV].

A theme is compatible with context variation: an AI-lab test can involve booking a public tour, directions around an exhibition, students planning a project and a lecture on research methodology. It should remain understandable without AI expertise. **Confidence: unverified; design recommendation**, informed by [F-L], [DEV].

## 2. All question types and response instructions

Six official umbrella families encompass the surface formats below. Question counts per block are officially variable. The **targets** are proposed practice defaults, not measured frequencies. Letter ranges, selection counts and question numbers must match each generated task. **Confidence: confirmed-official** [F-L]; template observations **confirmed-by-papers** [P-L].

| Type | Printed wording / instruction pattern | Inspected count; proposed target | Item-writing rule and evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single MCQ | `Choose the correct letter, A, B or C.` | 2 items, p.8; target 3–5. | Three options, one intended answer; question or sentence-stem form. [F-L] confirmed-official; [P-L] pp.8–10 confirmed-by-papers. |
| Multi-answer MCQ | `Choose TWO letters, {range}.` State actual number; allocate that many numbered responses for a paper-style set. | Official library-guide variant exists [BC-L]; complete printed count not audited here. Target 2–3 selections per set. | Exactly the requested number correct; record whether ordering is immaterial. Multi-selection confirmed-official [F-L]; exact target unverified/design recommendation. |
| Matching | Task-specific relationship question, followed by `Write the correct letter, A, B or C, next to questions 21-25.` The sample adds `You may choose any letter more than once.` Adapt numbers/range to the block. | 5 course options/3 categories, p.18; 4 hotel descriptions/5 options, p.22. Target 4–6. | Options may be reusable or unused depending on rubric. Match the stated relation, not merely the occurrence of the name. confirmed-by-papers [P-L] pp.18–25. |
| Plan labelling | `Label the plan below.` Then choose from the supplied lettered options; an extraction variant requires separate specimen verification. | 5 labels/9 options, p.26; target 4–6. | An entrance, rooms, doors and landmarks ground the speaker's directions. Label positions follow audio sequence. confirmed-by-papers [P-L] pp.26–28; spatial construct confirmed-official [F-L]. |
| Map labelling | `Label the map below.` Then specify the answer list. Word-limited map gaps were not verified in the inspected official material. | No additional map block audited; target 4–6. | Outdoor spatial relationships must determine unique locations. Do not imply north merely means top unless orientation is supplied. Format confirmed-official [F-L]; directive reconstructed; geometry gate is design recommendation. |
| Diagram/picture labelling | `Label the diagram below.` State selection/extraction contract. | No distinct equipment-picture block audited; target 3–5. | Relate audio description to visible components, avoiding dependency on specialist visual knowledge. Format confirmed-official [F-L]; wording reconstructed; target unverified. |
| Form completion | `Complete the form below.` + word/number limit. | 8 gaps, p.3; target 5–10. | Transactional fields with printed labels, units and prefilled facts; answers fit names/addresses/measurements. confirmed-by-papers [P-L] pp.3–7. |
| Note completion | `Complete the notes below.` + word/number limit. | 6 gaps, p.29; target 5–10. | Organized notes record natural main facts, not arbitrary deleted articles. confirmed-official [F-L]; sample confirmed-by-papers [P-L] pp.29–33. |
| Table completion | `Complete the table below.` + word/number limit. | 4 gaps, p.30; target 4–6. | Meaningful row/column categories; prevent ambiguity over which unit is already supplied. confirmed-by-papers [P-L] pp.30–33; Part 4 table variant [BC-L]. |
| Flow-chart completion | `Complete the flow-chart below.` + extraction limit, or lettered word/phrase-list instructions. | Biology-student list-selection variant indexed [HUB]; block count not audited. Target 3–5. | Arrow relationships and steps follow audio meaning; list mode and transcription mode are distinct. confirmed-official [F-L], [HUB]; targets unverified. |
| Summary completion | `Complete the summary below.` + extraction limit or specified option-list contract. | Format listed [HUB]; no standalone block counted. Target 4–6. | Connected summary of main information; unlike Reading's exception, maintain Listening answer chronology. confirmed-official [F-L], [HUB]; wording reconstructed. |
| Sentence completion | `Complete the sentences below.` + limit. | 4 gaps, two-word maximum, p.14; target 3–5. | Paraphrased sentences target important information/relations; the recorded answer fits unchanged. confirmed-official [F-L]; confirmed-by-papers [P-L] pp.14–17. |
| Short answer | `Answer the questions below.` + word/number limit. | 6 numbered slots in three two-answer questions, p.11; target 3–6 slots. | Facts, places, prices, times or specified sets; number of requested answers is explicit. confirmed-official [F-L]; paired key confirmed-by-papers [P-L] pp.11–13. |

### Word limits and routing

The official number-inclusive pattern is `NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER`. The historical form sample permits THREE words and/or a number; the sentence sample permits TWO words. One-word-only instructions are also legitimate practice contracts, but no rule says all recent Part 1/4 gaps must use that maximum. **Confidence: confirmed-official** [F-L]; sampled limits **confirmed-by-papers** [P-L] pp.3, 11, 14, 29–30; claimed fixed part-specific limits **unverified**.

Construct the complete response instruction from: action, allowed lexical/numeric material, maximum, one answer per gap, and numbered destination. “NO MORE THAN” is an upper bound, not an instruction to use exactly that many words. Do not apply an extraction limit to letter choices or mark the name of an option correct when a letter was requested. **Confidence: confirmed-official for upper bound** [F-L]; mode enforcement **unverified; design recommendation**, based on [P-L].

Historical sample booklets can retain an example and references to original paper pages even under a “2023” filename. Their task/key mechanisms are useful; those layout details must not be copied into a contemporary test. **Confidence: confirmed-by-papers** [P-L] pp.3, 29–30, qualified by **confirmed-official** [CHANGE-2020].

## 3. Structural rules and natural distractors

| Rule / pattern | Confidence | Evidence and authoring implication |
|---|---|---|
| Part 1 may include spelled names, address components, postcodes and quantities. | confirmed-by-papers | Shipping conversation [P-L] pp.4–7. A spelling event is a realistic option, not a compulsory quota. |
| The required completion word is heard without needing a grammatical transformation. Surrounding question text can paraphrase. | confirmed-official | [F-L]; motivation/modules/summer-school task [P-L] pp.14–17. |
| Wrong options can be mentioned aloud; hearing a word is not sufficient evidence that it answers the question. | confirmed-by-papers | Insurance conversation [P-L] pp.8–10 discusses all packages and then selects one. |
| Correction, rejection, changed decision and negation are legitimate distractor mechanisms. | confirmed-official partner guidance | [DISTRACTOR]. Do not force a correction into every item. |
| Past/future/current distinctions can disambiguate competing labels. | confirmed-by-papers | Library map [P-L] p.27: a moved art collection and a future tourist section compete with current facilities. |
| Numerically related figures can compete when the question asks for a total or final agreed amount. | confirmed-by-papers | Shipping value discussion [P-L] pp.5–7. Do not turn Listening into an unannounced arithmetic exam. |
| Map conventions should include a usable orientation/start point and visible landmarks; a compass is useful when cardinal directions are used, not universally obligatory. | confirmed-by-papers for library entrance/landmarks; unverified for universal geometry policy | [P-L] p.26; geometry consistency gate is a design recommendation. |
| Part 4 runs as one sustained academic monologue with no break between question sets. Preview the whole ten-question run before the talk. | confirmed-official partner teaching guidance, indexed only for no-break claim; preview implementation is a design recommendation | Monologue [F-L]; explicit no-break statement in [L-PART4], corroborated by [L-PART4-ALT]. Both direct downloads failed/redirected. Exact preview/silence durations remain unverified. |
| Scheduled reading/checking intervals are different from user-controlled pausing/replay. | confirmed-by-papers for scheduled interval; design recommendation for simulation behavior | A literal scheduled pause occurs in [P-L] p.31. [PAUSE-CONFLICT]'s “no pauses” is overbroad when read literally. |
| Specific questions need not all be separated by the same interval; let information density support realistic processing demands. | unverified; design recommendation | Timing/rate quotas absent from [F-L]; test balance [DEV]. |

### Audio construction and validation

The generation package should include audio, a verbatim transcript, speaker roles, question groups, preview/check timing and answer timestamps. Validate **rendered audio**, not merely the written script: synthesized voices can skip a number, mispronounce a spelling sequence or merge adjacent answers. Confirm one-play delivery, intelligibility, duration and meaningful accent variation. **Confidence: unverified; design recommendation**, operationalizing [F-L], [DEV].

Do not assign an official target such as “exactly 150 words per minute”, “350 words per part”, “one Canadian speaker in Part 3”, or “all ten Part 4 items are note completion”. No such universal public specification was verified. **Confidence: unverified**, [F-L], [BC-L].

## 4. Answer key and scoring

| Correctness rule | Confidence / sources |
|---|---|
| One mark per correct numbered response; no invented negative marks or partial semantic credit within a single gap. | confirmed-official [SCORE]; gap-credit policy is design recommendation |
| Incorrect spelling, over-limit responses and inappropriate plural forms can lose marks. | confirmed-official partner guidance [F-L], [SPELL] |
| A hyphenated form counts as one word; contracted forms are not targets in the described completion tasks. | confirmed-official [F-L] |
| Accept capitalization variation under a declared practice policy; block capitals are allowed. | confirmed-official partner guidance [CAPS]; normalization policy is design recommendation |
| Numeric and spelling variants are item-specific. The official key demonstrates metric conversion, British/American spellings and optional words. | confirmed-by-papers [P-L] p.7; do not extrapolate that every unit conversion is always accepted |
| A unit printed before/after the blank need not be entered again; where no unit is supplied, include one if required for meaning/key. | confirmed-by-papers form currency stem [P-L] p.3; partner guidance [SPELL]; stem-aware grading is design recommendation |
| Brackets denote optional elements; slashes denote alternatives; paired “either order” slots are scored as a set. | confirmed-by-papers [P-L] pp.7, 13 |
| Singular/plural variants are not globally interchangeable; some keys explicitly accept both. | confirmed-by-papers [P-L] p.17 versus accuracy guidance [SPELL] |

For every item, store canonical answer, allowed variants, word/number maximum, optional unit policy, evidence timestamp and rationale. For sets, define distinct membership, response cardinality and either-order scoring so duplicates cannot receive two marks. **Confidence: unverified; design recommendation**, informed by [P-L], [SCORE].

## 5. Repeated claims to reject or qualify

| Claim | Verdict | Confidence / source |
|---|---|---|
| Listening MCQ has four options like Reading. | Wrong for standard single-answer format: three. | confirmed-official [F-L], [F-R] |
| Part 1 always has a spelling question and all ten items are form completion. | Useful authoring pattern, not verified mandate. | unverified; varied Part 1 samples [P-L] pp.3, 8, 22 |
| Every wrong option must be spoken word-for-word. | No universal requirement; distractors can be semantic paraphrases. | unverified quota; paraphrase guidance [DISTRACTOR] |
| No audio pauses of any kind occur. | Overbroad; scheduled pauses exist, though candidates do not control them. | confirmed-by-papers [P-L] p.31; [PAUSE-CONFLICT] flagged |
| Ten transfer minutes apply to computer Listening. | Wrong delivery profile. | confirmed-official [GUIDE], [COMPUTER] |
| Part 4 must contain only ONE WORD ONLY note completion. | Unsupported; official Part 4 table sample exists. | confirmed-by-papers/index [BC-L]; quota unverified |
| Numerical/plural variants should always be accepted when the meaning seems close. | Overbroad; keyed exceptions and grammar determine correctness. | confirmed-by-papers [P-L], partner accuracy guidance [SPELL] |
