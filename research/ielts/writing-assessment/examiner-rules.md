# IELTS Academic Writing: rules, limits and prompt construction

Checked 11 October 2026. Confidence labels: **official** = directly supported by a partner publication; **examiner-claim** = attributable practitioner advice, not confirmed policy; **unverified** = no adequate public evidence located. An inference or proposed implementation is identified explicitly. Official preparation advice is not automatically an internal marking instruction.

## Scoring mechanics

| Rule | Confidence | Source and qualification |
|---|---|---|
| Assess each task independently using four equally weighted criteria; a task score is their average. | official | [IELTS scoring in detail](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/your-results/ielts-scoring-in-detail), Writing section. |
| Task 2 contributes twice as much as Task 1. | official | [Academic Writing format](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-writing). |
| Raw weighted mean: W = (T1 + 2×T2)/3, with T1=(TA+CC+LR+GRA)/4 and T2=(TR+CC+LR+GRA)/4. | official mechanics; algebraic inference | Derived from those two sources. Preserve raw precision. |
| IELTS reports whole/half bands. | official | [Official sample responses](https://ielts.org/cdn/computer-delivered-sample-tests-academic-writing/ielts-academic-writing-example-responses-to-parts-1-and-2-with-band-scores-and-examiner-comments.pdf), introductory page. |
| Exact internal Writing rounding sequence, intermediate truncation, and tie handling. | unverified | The [scoring page](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/your-results/ielts-scoring-in-detail) explicitly describes rounding for the overall four-skill score; its Writing paragraph does not specify these internal steps. Do not transfer that rule without qualification. |

Worked example: T1 criteria 6,6,6,6 give 6; T2 criteria 7,7,7,7 give 7; raw Writing mean = 20/3 = 6.6667. A proposed nearest-half estimate is 6.5, clearly labelled an estimate. T1=6.25 and T2=7 yield W=6.75; without a verified Writing tie rule, retain 6.75 and mark the reported-band estimate uncertain. Never round task averages first merely to simplify arithmetic.

## Length, format, relevance and authenticity

| Rule | Confidence | Source / implementation consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum 150 words in T1; 250 in T2. Longer responses have no automatic over-length penalty. Short answers can lack assessable evidence. | official | [Writing format](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-writing). |
| “One band deducted below the minimum”, universal word-deficit ladders, or a universal 250-word ceiling. | unverified | No such schedule found in the [current public format guidance](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-writing). Do not implement. |
| Notes and bullets are inappropriate; responses must be connected prose. Off-topic material and plagiarism are penalised. | official | [Writing format](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-writing). Exact numerical deductions are not given. |
| Headings/subheadings are inappropriate in a T2 essay. | official preparation guidance | [IDP Focus student book](https://info.ielts.idp.com/rs/561-EIU-022/images/IELTS%20Focus%20-%20AC%20Writing%20T2%20-%20Student%20updated.pdf), printed p13. Not evidence that a standalone title always costs a fixed band. |
| Copied rubric receives no credit. | official | [IELTS sample tasks](https://ielts.org/cdn/Sample-tests/ielts-academic-writing-sample-tasks-2023.pdf), T2 2B-A, comment p24. |
| Deduct copied introduction text from the assessed word count. | official example, not comprehensive counting manual | [IDP Focus](https://info.ielts.idp.com/rs/561-EIU-022/images/IELTS%20Focus%20-%20AC%20Writing%20T2%20-%20Student%20updated.pdf), printed p47. Preserve both total and adjusted counts; do not remove unavoidable topic words. |
| Hyphenated words count as one word. | official partner guidance | [British Council: British or American English](https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/blog/british-or-american-english-ielts), counting subsection. This is not a full Writing tokenisation specification. |
| Exact Writing treatment of contractions, numerals, dates, slash forms, titles, abbreviations, and all punctuation edge cases. | unverified | No complete public manual located; the [BC article](https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/blog/british-or-american-english-ielts) establishes only the hyphen point. Do not import Reading/Listening answer limits as Writing rules. |
| A stock phrase proves memorisation; all templates force TR4; elegant prose is evidence of plagiarism. | unverified | [Memorised-learning research](https://ielts.org/researchers/our-research/research-reports/the-effect-of-memorized-learning-on-the-writing-scores-of-chinese-ielts-test-takers) treats authenticity as a diagnostic problem, not phrase matching. |

Proposed counting convention: count whitespace-separated written tokens, strip punctuation-only tokens, count hyphenated compounds once, and identify copied passages separately. Report the convention and uncertain cases. It is a reproducible estimate, not examiner software. A near-threshold count should trigger review, not a manufactured deduction.

## Criterion limits and boundaries

The following compact paraphrases are **official**, based on the [May 2023 descriptor tables](https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-guides/ielts-writing-band-descriptors.pdf), physical pp3–5 and 7–9:

- T1 TA7 requires a clear overview; TA6 allows an attempted one. No clear overview prevents TA7, but does not cap the whole task at 6.
- T2 TR7 requires a developed position; TR6 permits unclear conclusions. TR3 describes no identifiable relevant position; TR2 no position. Do not assign every unclear stance the same ceiling.
- Band 1 applies to responses of at most 20 words. Band 0 covers no attempt, wholly non-English writing, or proven total memorisation.
- From 6 to 7: more complete development, sustained organisation, lexical precision and flexible complex grammar with frequent accurate sentences.
- From 7 to 8: stronger coverage, easier reading, wider precise language and mostly accurate sentences with occasional non-systematic errors.

Use the full criterion row, including limiting features; isolated strengths do not justify promotion. These summaries do not replace the original tables.

The [official process script 1C-B](https://ielts.org/cdn/Sample-tests/ielts-academic-writing-sample-tasks-2023.pdf#page=15) is a useful counterexample to a whole-task overview cap: its published task band is 7. Historical sample scores and revised criterion requirements need to be distinguished.

The [2020 IDP preparation book](https://info.ielts.idp.com/rs/561-EIU-022/images/IELTS%20Focus%20-%20AC%20Writing%20T2%20-%20Student%20updated.pdf), printed p15, gives a TR5 ceiling for answering only one part. Treat this as older official teaching guidance; for a current judgement apply the revised TR table and the actual extent of omission. It does not mean every unevenly developed essay is TR5.

## Prompt construction: evidence versus proposed settings

**Official format:** graph/chart/table/diagram summaries and essays answering a point of view, argument or problem; maps/plans and processes also appear in [IELTS preparation resources](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/preparation-resources/writing-test-resources). Multiple visuals are allowed in the [Academic format description for organisations](https://ielts.org/organisations/ielts-for-organisations/test-types/ielts-academic-test). These sources do not publish statistical data-density norms or item-writer specifications.

Verified exemplars in the [official 2023 sample PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/Sample-tests/ielts-academic-writing-sample-tasks-2023.pdf): p3 further-education bars across three periods, sex and study mode; p4 two audience series over a day; p5 brick manufacturing with stages and a drying branch. This is a small convenience sample, not a frequency survey.

The following are **unverified as IELTS norms**, proposed initial generator settings requiring review against a larger licensed Cambridge corpus:

| T1 type | Proposed density | Validation requirements |
|---|---|---|
| Line | 2–4 series; 5–8 time points | Label units/time; meaningful crossings, peaks or contrasting trends. |
| Bar | 4–6 categories; 2–3 groups | Distinguish counts from proportions; readable legend. |
| Pie | 2–3 charts; 4–6 slices each | Shares total 100%, allowing declared rounding. |
| Table | 4–6 rows; 3–5 numeric columns | Clear row/column meaning; enough variation for grouping. |
| Process | 6–9 major stages | Flow, branches and endpoints unambiguous; no invented causes needed. |
| Map | 2 dated plans; 5–8 salient changes | Consistent orientation/scale; label unchanged features too. |
| Mixed | 2 related visuals | Shared topic, compatible periods/units; cross-visual comparisons possible. |

**T1 instruction wording**, short exact excerpt from [the official samples](https://ielts.org/cdn/Sample-tests/ielts-academic-writing-sample-tasks-2023.pdf), p3:

> Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Retain 20-minute guidance and the 150-word minimum. Construct a neutral descriptive stem naming the visual, population/place, measure and period. Provide original data and an actual readable visual, rather than prose pretending to be a chart.

**T2 families** are planning categories, not a published exhaustive taxonomy. Exact short wording excerpts from [IDP Focus](https://info.ielts.idp.com/rs/561-EIU-022/images/IELTS%20Focus%20-%20AC%20Writing%20T2%20-%20Student%20updated.pdf), printed pp15–16:

> To what extent do you agree or disagree?

> Discuss both these views and give your own opinion.

Other exact patterns are linked to their original pages rather than reproduced at length:

| Family | Required response (paraphrase) | Original wording location |
|---|---|---|
| Agreement | Degree of agreement with a proposition | IDP Focus pp15–16; official sample 2A p6. |
| Discussion + opinion | Both stated views and the writer's judgement | IDP Focus p16. |
| Outweigh | Benefits, drawbacks and their relative weight | IDP Focus p16; official sample 2B p7. |
| Positive/negative development | Evaluate the development with a supported judgement | IDP Focus pp16,23. |
| Causes/solutions | Explain causes and feasible responses | IDP Focus pp16,24. |
| Advantages/disadvantages | Explain both, respecting any additional judgement request | IDP Focus p48, gap-year task. |
| Two direct questions | Answer each requested aspect; do not force it into an unrelated essay template | Parse the actual wording; this is a proposed generic routing category, not a separately verified official sample here. |

Use about 40 minutes and at least 250 words, with reasons and relevant examples. For an outweigh task, explaining both sides and a comparative judgement is supported by [IDP's official example guidance](https://ielts.idp.com/uae/about/news-and-articles/article-how-to-understand-task-2-writing-questions/en-gb); counting arguments does not establish which side is more important.

Generator review procedure (proposed): choose a family; specify every requested aspect; write one concise neutral setup; check it requires no specialist knowledge; check ambiguity, cultural accessibility and factual assumptions; test whether at least two defensible positions exist where appropriate; compare the wrapper with linked originals; obtain blind expert review against licensed authentic items. Do not advertise prompts as authentic past papers or “indistinguishable” until that claim is empirically tested.
