# IELTS Academic Writing descriptors: authoritative text index

Research checked 11 October 2026. Applies to Academic Task 1 and Task 2.

## Authoritative edition

The [official nine-page Writing band descriptor PDF](https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-guides/ielts-writing-band-descriptors.pdf) identifies its revision as May 2023. IELTS also announced the revision on [3 May 2023](https://ielts.org/news-and-insights/ielts-writing-band-descriptors-and-key-assessment-criteria). The [scales review report](https://ielts.org/researchers/our-research/research-reports/ielts-writing-scales-review-and-update-summary-overview) confirms operational introduction that month.

**Reproduction limitation:** this file is not a verbatim transcription. The requested complete copyrighted text cannot be reproduced here. The links below locate every band and every criterion in the original; load that original when applying the draft grader. Do not substitute this index or an older rubric for the actual descriptors.

## Loading and version checks for the future skill

Proposed implementation contract, not IELTS policy:

1. Obtain the original PDF through the official URL, retaining its source and revision date in the grading context.
2. Check bands 0–9, four columns per task, and the Academic/General Training distinction. Inspect the rendered table as well as extracted text; column interleaving can corrupt automatic extraction.
3. Preserve limiting features and modal language. Do not turn examples, possibilities or criterion-specific restrictions into universal whole-task caps.
4. If the PDF is unavailable, report that rubric verification failed. Give qualitative feedback, but do not claim an examiner-faithful score from this index alone.
5. Record the descriptor edition with every score. Older official sample marks remain useful historical anchors; they are not proof of criterion scores under the revised edition.
