# Sources and evidence register

Research checked 11 October 2026. Scope: standard IELTS Academic; Listening and Speaking are shared with General Training. This register records the public-source basis and the local Cambridge corpus used across the research reports.

## How to interpret the reports

- **confirmed-official**: explicitly stated in a partner's format, assessment or administration material. A partner preparation article is identified as guidance, rather than a confidential test specification.
- **confirmed-by-papers**: directly observed in an identified official sample task, transcript or answer key. It proves that construction is possible; it does **not** establish a universal rule or corpus frequency.
- **examiner-claim**: attributed examiner/author testimony, including dated research interviews. It is not automatically current examiner policy.
- **unverified**: no sufficiently strong confirmation, conflicting guidance, or a deliberately chosen app authoring target. Such targets are labelled **design recommendation** and are not presented as IELTS requirements.

Rule tables carry confidence and source links. Bracketed source IDs resolve to URLs within each report. In the checklist, **MUST** means a required property of the proposed practice generator, not necessarily an official IELTS mandate: additional quality gates are explicitly labelled **design recommendation**. **SHOULD** means a default with justified exceptions. No low-trust blog is used to establish a construction rule.

The Cambridge observation set contains four tests per book for Cambridge 12–20 (36 PDF-backed test sets), plus Cambridge 21's four Ppo question sets. Every aggregate is scoped to Cambridge 12–21; books 1–11 do not shape the counts, rules or ratios. The exact test-level question sequences, topics, keys, and printed-page/PDF-scan/Ppo-page locators are in [the Cambridge ledger](cambridge/corpus.md); aggregate counts and denominators are in [patterns.md](cambridge/patterns.md). The statistics characterize this retired-paper sample, not all current live IELTS tests.

The supplied source PDFs and audio remain in the private corpus. Research files record source locators, task labels, paraphrases and aggregate counts; they do not redistribute passage bodies, question/answer blocks, complete Writing prompts, sample essays or listening transcripts. The few language examples in [register.md](cambridge/register.md) are phrase-length. Public materials cannot reveal all confidential item-writer specifications, live item statistics, exact script permissions or version-specific band tables. Comparable difficulty still requires expert review and representative learner trials; the reports do not claim psychometric equivalence.
