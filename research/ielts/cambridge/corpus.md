# Cambridge Academic corpus ledger: IELTS 12–21

Research checked 11 October 2026. This ledger uses Cambridge 12–20 user-provided Academic PDFs as the primary evidence; Cambridge 21 is Ppo-only. Books 1–11 are excluded from all profiles and counts. The complete Cambridge 13, 15 and 20 copies supersede earlier partial PDFs and scraped text; Ppo remains a cross-check. The private corpus is never redistributed.

## Reading the ledger

Each test citation gives the book's printed page range, except Cambridge 20, where unstable printed footers require PDF scan-page ranges. A test page range covers its Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking prompts. Ppo IDs are direct page links and identify its Listening (L), Reading (R) or Writing (W) page. Local Cambridge PDFs/OCR are primary for Cambridge 12–20; Ppo is an unofficial cross-check. The supplied C20 PDF/OCR set does not expose a readable answer-key section, so its Reading status-key statistics use Ppo R310–R313. Cambridge 21 is Ppo-only.

Question abbreviations: TFNG = TRUE/FALSE/NOT GIVEN; YNNG = YES/NO/NOT GIVEN; MCQ = one answer from A–C; M2 = choose two letters; Match = match items to a supplied option bank; Notes/Form/Table/Flow = completion; Map = map or plan labelling. Ranges name scored question numbers. Printed completion caps are reproduced in the task notes when legible; the common variants are ONE WORD ONLY, ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER, and NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS. Matching/MCQ blocks use their printed letter-count instructions. Listening scripts name roles or speakers but do not systematically label voice gender or accent; none is inferred.

Reading word lengths are rough page-layout estimates of about 650–1,000 words per passage, not machine counts. The scans and OCR do not support reliable exact counts. Genre tags describe the adapted text's visible form (feature, explainer, profile, argument); they are not claims about an unverified original publication. Passage titles are listed as printed when legible; unreadable titles remain marked.

Task 2 entries give the prompt family and a concise topic/obligation paraphrase, with the source located by the test's printed page range (or Ppo page for Cambridge 21). Exact prompt wording is not transcribed across the ledger. The separate [register notes](register.md) use only a few phrase-length examples; no passage body, answer choices, sample answer or listening transcript is reproduced.
